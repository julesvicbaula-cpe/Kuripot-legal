Kuripot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Kuripot ("the app") is a personal expense tracker made by Jules Vic Baula ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

Information the app collects

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to create an account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; I cannot see or read your password.
  • Expense data you enter: expense titles, amounts, categories, dates, optional notes, and your monthly budget.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or device advertising ID. It does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

How the information is used

Your information is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to store your expenses and budget, and to sync them across the devices where you are signed in. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as a service provider that hosts the data for the app. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Security rules ensure each signed-in user can only read and write their own data. Google's handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

Sharing

I do not share your information with other people or companies, except Google as the hosting provider described above, or if required by law.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Deleting your data and account

  • In the app: open the account menu (the person icon at the top right), choose Delete account, and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, expenses and budget.
  • Without the app: see how to request deletion online.

You can also delete individual expenses at any time inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
