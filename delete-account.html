Delete your Kuripot account

This page explains how to delete your Kuripot account and the data linked to it.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kuripot and sign in.
  2. Tap the person icon at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email julesvicbaula@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. Use the subject "Delete my Kuripot account". I will delete your account and data and confirm by email, normally within 14 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details).
  • All your expenses (titles, amounts, categories, dates, notes).
  • Your monthly budget.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. No data is kept afterwards, except for any short-lived backups that Google's infrastructure may hold for a limited time before they are overwritten.
